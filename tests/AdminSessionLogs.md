## Slot and view audits — 2026-10-27

Regular self-slot requests already used the central audit path. They now appear as **Slotting** (`slot`) events rather than admin events, with slot/role and selected/released/rejected outcome. Each Logs activation resets its read audit; the next authorized read records view opened once, while filters, refreshes and background polling do not repeatedly audit the same open view.
