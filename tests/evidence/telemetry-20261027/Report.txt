LIMITS
This is useful playback source data, not a working viewer or full animation-graph save. Bone geometry is sampled at 1 Hz; short transitions may be missed. Exact projectile impacts/flight, grenade detonations and AI firing are not captured. Damage-to-shot/death correlation remains unknown when native evidence supplies no link. Assets still need a separate browser mapping/conversion pipeline. Disk writes are synchronous, retention requires operator policy, and a 128-player benchmark is still required before broad deployment.

Independent review approved the documented slice. Final Workbench remains open with the test addon; recording is stopped.
