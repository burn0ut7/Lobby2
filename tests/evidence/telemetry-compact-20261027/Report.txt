COMPACT TELEMETRY — 27 October 2026

Implemented schema 2: no skeleton, pose, bone matrix, weapon matrix, or ragdoll tracks. Position and native Euler orientation replace root matrices. Velocity remains useful for playback motion; redundant scalar speed is derived. Animation inputs retain stance, command, ADS/raised/reload/sprint/freelook flags, lean, stance height, movement and independent aim/look. Equipment selection is emitted only on change. Native gear/faction snapshots remain necessary for appearance reconstruction.

Numeric inputs are compared before JSON construction, against the last emitted values, with 0.001 vector/scalar thresholds; small accumulated changes eventually emit. Discrete values remain exact. States use three decimal places and refresh at five seconds. They are complete snapshots, not dependent deltas. previousSampleMs identifies the actual preceding successful observation so playback holds stationary intervals rather than sliding across suppressed records. Failed observations do not advance that clock. Interpolation must respect discontinuity and native angle wrapping. No playback viewer or asset conversion was added.

Deaths record final position/orientation and end that actor's sampling. Unattributed deaths of already tracked AI also terminate sampling, with despawn reason dead and the final available state. No corpse physics is retained. Active actors are separate from a bounded retired-identity cache; IDs never reuse. The scheduler waits for the next due actor instead of scanning all actors every frame; the previous 16-actor ceiling is removed. Its 2 ms post-actor budget remains soft. JSON batches flush by time/bytes/record count. Recording quota is now an explicit authenticated parameter, 64–8192 MiB, default 1024 MiB; this is not global disk retention.
